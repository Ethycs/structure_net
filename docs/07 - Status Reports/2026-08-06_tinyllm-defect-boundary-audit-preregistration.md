Are the extra directions semantic geometry, or only decoder-boundary margin

## Intervention

Let `D_r` be the near-rank projection and `D` the exact defect. Causally
interpolate only the omitted component:

```text
state(t) = propagated + D_r + t (D - D_r)
t in {0, 0.125, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.875, 1}.
```

Also add each omitted source singular direction individually and patch the
previously sufficient rank as a positive control.

## Boundary measurements

For near versus exact posteriors report:

- prediction disagreement, introduced-error, and rescued-error rates;
- fraction of disagreements between adjacent circular bins;
- mean, median, 95th-percentile, and maximum posterior-moment displacement in
  bin widths;
- exact top-two log-probability margins for agreeing and disagreeing examples;
- fraction of disagreements contained in the bottom quartile of exact margins;
- first interpolation coefficient at which each near disagreement matches the
  exact prediction;
- circular alignment, winding, sampling resolution, exact-bin accuracy, and
  Fisher preservation along the path.

## Primary classification

A failed cell is `boundary_only` only if all hold:

1. near/exact prediction disagreement is at most `0.10`;
2. at least `0.90` of disagreements are between adjacent circular bins;
3. mean moment displacement is at most `0.125` bin widths;
4. circular-alignment loss relative to exact is at most `0.005`.

A cell is `continuous_map_distortion` if alignment loss exceeds `0.01` or mean
moment displacement exceeds `0.25` bin widths. Remaining cells are
`distributed_boundary_sensitivity`.

The hypothesis requires all three registered failing cells to be
`boundary_only`. The exact and sufficient-rank controls must pass while each
near-rank primary cell fails its frozen task gate. Controls cannot rescue a
failed mechanism classification.

At campaign level, predecessor reproduction, exact decomposition, and endpoint
controls must pass in both checkpoints, and all three failure cells must be
`boundary_only`. Matched cells where the near rank already passed are secondary
controls and cannot rescue a failed registered cell.

## Method boundaries

The audit conditions on already observed near-rank failures and does not
independently replicate quotient formation. It diagnoses the frozen exact-bin
decoder boundary, not an intrinsic semantic dimension. Interpolation supplies
the exact omitted residual and therefore tests causal effect rather than
independent computability. Singular directions are checkpoint-local and the
patched path can be off the natural activation manifold.
