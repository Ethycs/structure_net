## What remains established

The evidence supports two different causal regimes.

### Cover-bearing raw models

For stable degree-two checkpoints, branch-bearing cover states feed a
distributed multi-head attention operation whose exact Reynolds/Jensen defect
creates a compact task-sufficient neutral carrier at a localized cut. The full
residual does not become a quotient, and the local mechanism is not universal
across degree three.

### Architecturally structured models

Once an observed calibration fixes the gauge, analytic and learned-equivariant
front ends expose a causally sufficient quotient before attention in `5/5`
checkpoints on both shifts. The unchanged transformer is already task-closed
on that projection, although natural residual fiber differences persist and
grow.

These regimes are compatible. Nonlinear invariant synthesis is needed when
the model receives a branch-bearing cover; it is not required when the front
end has already restricted the representation to a task-sufficient quotient.

For known independent symmetric edge noise, exhaustive `3^7` enumeration
matches `lambda(p)=(1-3p/2)^7` below `1.2e-15`; the current joint scalar gate
requires per-edge `p <= 9.525e-6`. Missing/partial and known-noise training are
therefore closed. Only a statistically identifiable observation-dependent or
unknown uncertainty law that can beat a fixed or low-dimensional adaptive
estimator can reopen scope; TinyLLM remains excluded by default.

The exact soft interface for that remaining uncertainty is now known. Across
all 2,080 denominator-63 C3 error posteriors and 2,048 phases, the complex
character moment `m=sum q_e exp(-2pi i e/3)` reconstructs the full posterior,
factorizes the Bayes cosine and squared-error risk, and strictly improves on a
MAP hard connection at every nonvertex posterior. All coordinate covariance
gates pass, and posterior averaging of the three hard counterfactual outputs
matches direct `z m` injection through all ten frozen module/shift cells within
`2.38e-7`. Do not relearn transport, the sensor, or the continuation. At most,
design a compact typed estimator of `m` after an identifiable unknown
uncertainty law is declared and shown to beat fixed-law, global-posterior, and
low-dimensional adaptive controls. This interface result alone licenses no
training.

A repository-wide training-license audit found no qualifying uncertainty
stream: current artifacts expose exact connections, hard controls, analytic
noise, or deterministic posterior values, but no repeated edge reads,
per-example reliability evidence, unknown error law, or real calibration
outcomes. Posterior-estimator training is therefore a formal no-go rather than
an unimplemented next run. Resume only with a new data/observation contract and
begin with identifiability, oracle, global-posterior, and low-dimensional
adaptive controls.

The external `New Graph Architecture` traces do not reopen that license.
Their confidence and uncertainty values are endogenous classifier/prototype
scores, their Beta posterior is a global FSM-legality mask, and
`edge_traversed` records consecutive predicted product-graph states rather
than repeated reads of a latent connection. The archived StructureNet notes
also contain no connection-error calibration stream. The later E38/E39/E49
experiments add protocol-state calibration, a perfect finite-rule verifier,
and globally declared POMDP emission drift respectively; none supplies paired
per-example observations and latent C3 connection errors. Mapping any of these
sources to C3 holonomy would invent the observation law after the estimator
interface was known, so the no-go remains in force.

## Gate before any new training

A new campaign MUST name which row above changes scope and pass these checks
before model optimization:

1. prove or exhaustively verify that the target descends through the declared
   observation relation;
2. construct the analytic positive-control projection or solution;
3. declare the group action, gauge anchor, sign, channel order, normalization,
   task metric, fusion rule, and downstream embedding convention;
4. retain matched data, minibatch, optimizer, and task controls;
5. preregister both representation and frozen causal-continuation endpoints;
6. use at least five seeds and require a joint seedwise gate across composition
   and extrapolation;
7. stop before training if the analytic contract or target-changing control is
   invalid.
