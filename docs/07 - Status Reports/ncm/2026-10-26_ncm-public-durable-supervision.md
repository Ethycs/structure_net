# Keep the resumed epoch independent of conversation lifetime

The original training process disappeared with the interrupted tool session.
The qualified interruption recovery preserves that unknown exit and its
uncommitted tail. Launch attempt2 through the existing user systemd manager,
using unit `structure-net-ncm-public-epoch1-attempt2.service`, the same locked
Pixi command, physical GPU1 and explicit deterministic environment. Use no
automatic restart. Keep the service loaded after exit and retain stdout/stderr
in a separate supervision directory on `/data2`.

A successful `systemd-run` command proves only that the service was launched.
It is not a successful training receipt. Observe `ActiveState`, `SubState`,
`Result`, `ExecMainCode`, `ExecMainStatus`, `ExecMainPID`, `InvocationID` and
`NRestarts` before deciding that training is terminal. Successful training
requires a terminal successful service state, an ordinary exit with status0,
zero service restarts and the complete fingerprint-matched1692-update result.

The eventual receipt must distinguish the launch tool's status, the observed
training-process status, and the status of the tool that verified that terminal
state. Bind the service identity, observation, producer and result. A lost chat
handle cannot establish either success or failure of this detached service.
Keep the unit's recorded status until the terminal evidence has been captured.

After the first resumed update, compare its recorded learning rate, losses,
gradient norm, scaler, IDs, positions, populations and corruption counts with
the original attempt's update257. Timing and allocator peaks may differ. This
is a restart-control comparison, not task accuracy or a new optimizer update.
No DEV sample or sealed final case is used by supervision or recovery checks.
