# Source guard for the prepared catalog union

Run an exploratory CPU-only source audit of all 208,631 indexed pairs before
admitting a new mixed-domain training experiment. Preserve the original union.
Use the existing normalized-equality or at least 32 common distinct word
five-grams with at least 80 percent containment of the shorter text rule.

Compare every distinct TRAIN source to all 128 open application DEV sources,
64 reserved component sources, 320 public development sources, and 128 recovered
label-diagnostic sources. Check the unchanged reserved metadata for all 512
original protected components, including sealed final cases, without opening or
hashing final-test source text. Add the open cohorts' source/group/family/turn
identities to that protected metadata.

Join original source components, groups, normalized family IDs, turn hashes and
exact source hashes across the combined TRAIN data. A direct metadata or text
conflict excludes the whole connected group from the derived eligibility map.
Retain the original pairs, contexts and failure evidence. Report complete counts,
domain-specific exclusions, direct conflicts and exclusions caused by grouping.
Do not create new validation splits or relabel examples.

This checks the combined TRAIN population against fixed open held-out cohorts
and reserved metadata. It does not claim an exhaustive comparison of all TRAIN
pairs with one another, an arbitrary semantic duplicate detector, or a full-text
comparison against the sealed final sources. It makes no quality or speed claim
and does not itself admit training. A new mixture still needs a defined training
protocol after current candidate evaluation.

Require three source-alias/group-propagation CPU tests before launch. Freeze the
producer and exact original inputs in the new append-only root
`20261028_combined_prepared_source_guard_v1`; use locked Pixi ncm. Record actual
terminal status, preserve failures, and inspect the complete result before any
consumer uses its eligibility map.
