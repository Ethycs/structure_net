## Verdict

The most economical interpretation of the accumulated evidence is not that a
TinyLLM globally erases a nuisance fiber. It is that the model:

1. carries a symmetry-typed, branch-bearing cover;
2. combines charged information across several attention heads;
3. writes a small quotient-sufficient neutral carrier at a localized causal
   front; and
4. converts that continuous carrier into task bins through a separately
   fallible readout.

The surrounding residual can retain branch information after the task no
longer needs it. Therefore the primary interpretability object should be a
**localized quotient-sufficient subspace and its synthesis operator**, not a
claim that the entire hidden state is a quotient.

For cross-cohort-stable degree-two checkpoints without a quotient-restricting
front end, the present empirical model is especially concrete:

```text
calibrated observation / identifiable target
    -> branch-bearing C2 carrier
    -> distributed block-0 multi-head attention
    -> exact nonlinear Reynolds/Jensen defect
    -> 2--3 dimensional continuous semantic carrier
    -> scalar boundary calibration
    -> degree-two task output.
```

The calibrated structured d8/N3 systems instantiate a second, shorter regime:

```text
observed calibration fixes the gauge
    -> analytic or equivariant front end admits an observed C2 action
    -> its within-example Reynolds twirl is task-sufficient before block-0 attention
    -> attention and MLP are already task-closed on that projection
    -> the unused fiber complement may persist and grow in the natural residual.
```

Thus distributed Reynolds/Jensen synthesis is a mechanism for cover-bearing
models, not a universal requirement after architectural quotient formation.

This direction unifies the strongest positive results while respecting the
many failed stronger hypotheses. It also yields shorter experiments: most next
questions can be answered by frozen-checkpoint interventions before training a
new architecture.

## What the evidence rules out

The following should no longer lead the program without a materially new
falsifier:

- **Global residual quotient:** mature states can remain strongly
  branch-decodable while exact orbit averaging is task-preserving.
- **Output topology as proof:** winding, persistent `H1`, or posterior
  alignment can pass while exact-bin accuracy, fiber contraction, or causal
  sufficiency fails.
- **Probe chance as proof:** a nuisance shift can erase both branch and semantic
  base, producing apparent invariance by information loss.
- **Whole-residual barycenter as proof:** in the valid raw d8 checkpoint, exact
  cosine-fiber averaging improves task metrics but changes the posterior beyond
  the locked preservation gate at every cut. Query-only averaging is exactly
  equivalent at final post-MLP, so token-locus restriction cannot create a
  mature front.
- **Average-loss improvement as proof:** at that same final query, generic
  logit-space Jensen convexity supplies at least the entire cross-entropy gain
  in both shifts; final layer normalization is adverse outside range, and a
  target-changing control obeys the same inequality. Lower mean loss after
  averaging is not evidence for semantic pairing or quotient closure.
- **Mostly task-null final-query fiber chord:** the exact local answer-map kernel
  is task-preserving and specific, but contains only a minority of the paired
  chord. Its rank-15 complement contains most of the displacement and explains
  the full output change, despite the two sheets having the same scalar target.
- **Hidden scalar quotient at the final query:** the rank-1 semantic tangent is
  exactly nested in the posterior tangent and task-specific, but its kernel
  fails both contraction and frozen scalar preservation on both declared
  shifts. A lower-dimensional endpoint does not rescue raw Euclidean closure.
- **Shape claims above a failed noise-dose control:** at `sigma=0.05`, the
  analytic arm fails the isotropic natural-utility positive control in all five
  seeds. Anisotropic and biased differences at that dose are therefore not
  evidence for or against noise-law symmetry, even though action and twirl are
  conditionally task-preserving relative to each noisy identity.
- **Reflection asymmetry as the single noise failure:** after selecting a common
  isotropic utility-valid dose without asymmetric outcomes, zero-mean lab
  anisotropy passes `4/5` analytic and `5/5` learned checkpoints. The law with
  a persistent lab-frame mean fails both populations, so bias—not reflection
  asymmetry in general—is the implicated property.
- **Penalty-first quotient construction:** block-1 base/adversary control
  optimized its local objectives but worsened fresh-probe branch leakage and
  failed extrapolation.
- **Symmetry without identifiability:** an equivariant front end cannot recover
  a target that does not descend through the observation gauge.
- **Equivariance as gauge fixing:** five exact calibrated-equivariant front ends
  divide into two sign classes, and even the closest within-class charts fail
  every extrapolation swap. The group action alone does not select a portable
  causal interface.
- **Clean linear deck descrambling:** held-out transport and invariant-component
  branch controls failed at every residual cut.
- **A privileged head or scalar write:** one/two-head and rank-one hypotheses
  failed across the stable degree-two checkpoints.
- **A small absolute quotient writer:** phase-only Fourier capacity through
  order 40, the observed calibration packet, and a rank-three chart of the
  propagated barycenter all fail complete frozen checkpoint gates.
- **A post-hoc three-coordinate task-phase representation:** the selected
  order-four writer is nearly first-harmonic but fails exact `C16` subspace
  closure in every checkpoint.
- **One nuisance-invariant rank-two projector field:** after repairing an
  invalid specificity control, only seed 29 passes every exact paired tail
  gate; seeds 7 and 53 fail every acquisition-action cell.
- **A universal low-order or landscape normal form:** quadratic truncation,
  radius thresholds, autonomous Koopman closure, and Morse structure were
  checkpoint-, shift-, or cohort-dependent.
- **Decoder-boundary-specific activation fitting:** ordinary ordered geometric
  directions repaired the near-rank states as well as boundary-weighted ones.

These are useful eliminations. They say the model's stable mechanism lives
between two bad extremes: it is neither a whole-state invariant nor a single
neuron/head/direction.

## Unifying object: a causal quotient chart

For an exact task fiber `h_j` and a residual sublayer `F_l`, retain the measured
operator

```text
chi_l = mean_j F_l(h_j) - F_l(mean_j h_j).
```

At the first cut where patching `F_l(mean h) + chi_l` makes the frozen
continuation pass, factor `chi_l` into:

- group type or harmonic;
- contributing head coalition;
- checkpoint-local ordered activation subspace;
- continuous task coordinate carried by that subspace; and
- final discrete readout calibration.

That record is a **causal quotient chart**. A chart is accepted only when its
unchanged intervention transfers across declared shifts and fresh orbit
cohorts. It need not claim that the orthogonal residual is nuisance-free.

This gives the project a stable unit of comparison across seeds, degrees, and
architectures. Neuron identities may permute, but group type, causal cut,
carrier rank, harmonic content, and readout relation can still agree.

The cross-seed swap tightens the definition: a shared group type is not yet a
shared chart. A portable chart must also declare its observed anchor, sign,
channel order, normalization, task metric, neutral fusion, and downstream
embedding convention. These are part of the causal interface rather than
post-hoc alignment freedoms.
