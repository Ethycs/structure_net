# TinyLLM unifying interpretability direction

**Status:** PROGRAM SYNTHESIS — evidence-backed direction, not a new measured result  
**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Scope:** completed TinyLLM geometry, invariance, degree, and frozen-checkpoint
causal studies through the fixed-gauge and propagated-context writer-capacity
decompositions, local continuation and nuisance-transport audits, source
task-covector portability, task-activation scalar sensing, and cross-seed
symmetry-feature swap, plus the inference-only joint-error, complete-system,
and orientation-reference robustness curves, frozen readout decomposition,
noisy-reference readout recalibration, repeated reference acquisition,
reference-path residual transport, and the posterior-coordinate transport
rank ladder

## Verdict

The most economical interpretation of the accumulated evidence is not that a
TinyLLM globally erases a nuisance fiber. It is that the model:

1. carries a symmetry-typed, branch-bearing cover;
2. combines charged information across several attention heads;
3. writes a small quotient-sufficient neutral carrier at a localized causal
   front; and
4. converts that continuous carrier into task bins through a separately
   fallible readout.

The surrounding residual can retain branch information after the task no
longer needs it. Therefore the primary interpretability object should be a
**localized quotient-sufficient subspace and its synthesis operator**, not a
claim that the entire hidden state is a quotient.

For the cross-cohort-stable degree-two checkpoints, the present empirical model
is especially concrete:

```text
calibrated observation / identifiable target
    -> branch-bearing C2 carrier
    -> distributed block-0 multi-head attention
    -> exact nonlinear Reynolds/Jensen defect
    -> 2--3 dimensional continuous semantic carrier
    -> scalar boundary calibration
    -> degree-two task output.
```

This direction unifies the strongest positive results while respecting the
many failed stronger hypotheses. It also yields shorter experiments: most next
questions can be answered by frozen-checkpoint interventions before training a
new architecture.

## What the evidence rules out

The following should no longer lead the program without a materially new
falsifier:

- **Global residual quotient:** mature states can remain strongly
  branch-decodable while exact orbit averaging is task-preserving.
- **Output topology as proof:** winding, persistent `H1`, or posterior
  alignment can pass while exact-bin accuracy, fiber contraction, or causal
  sufficiency fails.
- **Probe chance as proof:** a nuisance shift can erase both branch and semantic
  base, producing apparent invariance by information loss.
- **Penalty-first quotient construction:** block-1 base/adversary control
  optimized its local objectives but worsened fresh-probe branch leakage and
  failed extrapolation.
- **Symmetry without identifiability:** an equivariant front end cannot recover
  a target that does not descend through the observation gauge.
- **Equivariance as gauge fixing:** five exact calibrated-equivariant front ends
  divide into two sign classes, and even the closest within-class charts fail
  every extrapolation swap. The group action alone does not select a portable
  causal interface.
- **Clean linear deck descrambling:** held-out transport and invariant-component
  branch controls failed at every residual cut.
- **A privileged head or scalar write:** one/two-head and rank-one hypotheses
  failed across the stable degree-two checkpoints.
- **A small absolute quotient writer:** phase-only Fourier capacity through
  order 40, the observed calibration packet, and a rank-three chart of the
  propagated barycenter all fail complete frozen checkpoint gates.
- **A post-hoc three-coordinate task-phase representation:** the selected
  order-four writer is nearly first-harmonic but fails exact `C16` subspace
  closure in every checkpoint.
- **One nuisance-invariant rank-two projector field:** after repairing an
  invalid specificity control, only seed 29 passes every exact paired tail
  gate; seeds 7 and 53 fail every acquisition-action cell.
- **A universal low-order or landscape normal form:** quadratic truncation,
  radius thresholds, autonomous Koopman closure, and Morse structure were
  checkpoint-, shift-, or cohort-dependent.
- **Decoder-boundary-specific activation fitting:** ordinary ordered geometric
  directions repaired the near-rank states as well as boundary-weighted ones.

These are useful eliminations. They say the model's stable mechanism lives
between two bad extremes: it is neither a whole-state invariant nor a single
neuron/head/direction.

## Unifying object: a causal quotient chart

For an exact task fiber `h_j` and a residual sublayer `F_l`, retain the measured
operator

```text
chi_l = mean_j F_l(h_j) - F_l(mean_j h_j).
```

At the first cut where patching `F_l(mean h) + chi_l` makes the frozen
continuation pass, factor `chi_l` into:

- group type or harmonic;
- contributing head coalition;
- checkpoint-local ordered activation subspace;
- continuous task coordinate carried by that subspace; and
- final discrete readout calibration.

That record is a **causal quotient chart**. A chart is accepted only when its
unchanged intervention transfers across declared shifts and fresh orbit
cohorts. It need not claim that the orthogonal residual is nuisance-free.

This gives the project a stable unit of comparison across seeds, degrees, and
architectures. Neuron identities may permute, but group type, causal cut,
carrier rank, harmonic content, and readout relation can still agree.

The cross-seed swap tightens the definition: a shared group type is not yet a
shared chart. A portable chart must also declare its observed anchor, sign,
channel order, normalization, task metric, neutral fusion, and downstream
embedding convention. These are part of the causal interface rather than
post-hoc alignment freedoms.

## Shortest decisive experiment ladder

### 1. Separate the continuous carrier from bin calibration

This diagnostic is complete. Its provenance-valid post-outcome corrective
replay selected ranks 2/3/3 and passed every held-out continuous and
scalar-calibrated discrete gate. Use a three-dimensional typed neutral carrier as the
conservative architecture budget for this cohort, with seed 7 requiring only
two measured directions, and treat seed 29's fifth singular direction as
readout margin rather than semantic dimension.

### 2. Test cross-seed representation type, not cross-seed neurons

This experiment is complete. Source-only paired alignment found two
near-perfect canonical axes and passed all coordinate-transfer and shuffled
specificity gates, but transported states passed zero causal cells. The third
canonical correlation was only `0.128--0.281`, and small transport residuals
moved the posterior moment enough to fail.

The initial geometric suggestion was:

```text
shared two-axis semantic core
    + checkpoint-local low-variance correction
    + support-sensitive scalar calibration.
```

The frozen carrier-Jacobian audit is now complete and rejects the third-axis
term as a universal causal explanation. Local Jacobians explain `0.983--0.994`
of signed transport error with perfect sign agreement, but axis 3 dominates
`0/6` directed pairs. Axes 1--2 dominate `2/6`; the remaining four are mixed.
The corrected update is:

```text
shared statistical carrier geometry
    + checkpoint-conditioned local task metric
    = checkpoint-stratified causal atlas.
```

The completed transport and Jacobian studies jointly test whether held-out
interventions expressed in carrier coordinates preserve:

- `C2` transformation type;
- circular moment and degree;
- causal task sufficiency; and
- composition/extrapolation behavior.

Unconstrained affine alignment did not rescue causal transport, and shuffled
orbit pairing failed decisively. The common two-axis relation is real but does
not by itself define a decoder-compatible causal chart.

### 3. Test group-anchored task-metric transport

This experiment is complete. It kept the exact `C2` orbit membership,
source/target carrier bases, fit cohorts, and 24 held-out cells fixed, then
weighted affine-map fitting by the frozen target pullback Fisher metric without
held-out adaptation.

Every numerical metric, target-control, predecessor-replay, and shuffled
specificity gate passed. Task-metric transport made seeds 7 and 53 causally
interchangeable in both directions and all eight held-out cells, roughly
halving their mean moment error. The global claim nevertheless failed:
coordinate transport passed `3/6`, continuous causal transport `2/6`, and
baseline dominance `2/6`. All eight extrapolation cells involving seed 29
failed.

The update is therefore pairwise rather than global:

```text
shared C2 irrep type
    + one 7/53 task-metric gauge class
    + a support-relative seed-29 chart.
```

Per preregistration, stop post-hoc carrier alignment. Portable coordinates must
now be fixed during training rather than recovered with a more flexible map.

### 4. Test the analytic fixed gauge before training

This positive control and its oracle decomposition are complete. The observed
neutral `C2` carrier predicts held-out rank-three coordinates strongly, but its
linear writer passes only `3/12` cells and no complete checkpoint. The
observation contract narrowly misses its mean-error ceiling, so the locked
oracle diagnostic replaced the observed carrier with exact
`(cos(2 phi), sin(2 phi), 1)` coordinates.

All oracle, primary-replay, and target-control contracts pass. Nevertheless,
observed-fit/oracle-evaluated and oracle-fit/oracle-evaluated writers both fail
all three checkpoint gates. The result is not sensor-limited. Since the oracle
carrier parameterizes the declared quotient and direct rank-three patches
pass, the restriction is now the quotient-to-write map: linear and
nuisance-blind is insufficient.

### 5. Test whether architectural equivariance fixes the chart

This frozen intervention is complete. Every one of the five calibrated
front ends satisfies the exact acquisition-group contract, but direct
cross-seed swaps pass `0/20` feature pairs and `0/20` scalar pairs. The
features divide into sign classes `{7, 17}` and `{29, 41, 53}`. Within the
closest same-sign pairs, eight composition cells pass, but all 40
extrapolation cells fail.

The conclusion is not that symmetry failed. It is that symmetry alone leaves
an internal gauge and checkpoint-coadapted continuation. The next learned
architecture must fix the observed sign anchor, channel order, normalization,
metric, neutral fusion, and typed embedding rather than merely repeating an
equivariance constraint.

### 6. Resolve write curvature versus invariant context

This nested frozen writer-capacity experiment is complete. It reused the same
checkpoints and cells and compared:

```text
exact quotient carrier
    -> low-order Fourier neutral writer
    -> quotient + observed invariant context writer
    -> unchanged frozen continuation.
```

Every replay, oracle, sheet-context, target-control, and shuffled-specificity
contract passes. Orders 2 and 4 lower aggregate mean error from `0.2112` to
`0.1752` and `0.1733` bins, but pass only `2/12` cells and no checkpoint. The
equal-width order-40 and order-4-by-calibration writers overfit: their held-out
mean errors worsen to `0.4462` and `0.3136` bins. All three checkpoints are
`unresolved_writer_limited`.

The locked follow-up then replaced observed calibration with a source-only
rank-three PCA chart of the actual propagated Reynolds barycenter. Those three
directions capture `0.939--0.961` of source variance and satisfy every
numerical, replay, target-control, and shuffled-specificity contract. Context
reduces mean error most for seed 29, from `0.2317` to `0.1566` bins, but every
best context arm passes only one of four cells and no checkpoint. All three are
`small_writer_insufficient`. Parameter-matched phase controls through order 18
also fail.

The no-fit local continuation audit is now complete. All 768 orbit-local
Jacobians have rank two. Tangent-only correction passes all 12 held-out cells,
while the complementary rank-one kernel moves the output by at most `0.00021`
bins on average per checkpoint. No norm-matched random tangent policy passes a
checkpoint. The causal coordinate error is therefore localized to the local
task tangent without another writer fit.

The registered stable-metric gate nevertheless fails `0/3`: minimum cross-cell
leading-direction cosine is only `0.759--0.811`. The relevant object is a
state/support-conditioned metric field, not one fixed checkpoint-level plane.

Two scalar-angle audits independently start from the activation-conditioned
`context_m04` writer and the older fixed-gauge order-4 writer. Their tangent
patches also pass every cell in all three checkpoints and explain
`0.964--0.997` of signed task error locally. Each full hypothesis still passes
only `2/3` because a different conservative `0.125`-bin specificity margin is
missed in one checkpoint. This triangulates task-tangent sufficiency while
reinforcing that its orientation and uniqueness are state- and writer-local.

The cheapest task-phase group audit is also complete. The current rank-three
order-four writer has first-harmonic-plus-constant overlap `0.988--0.997` and
is far more structured than random, but its maximum `C16` orbit obstruction is
`0.089--0.201`; exact writer closure passes `0/3`. A task-phase covector cannot
therefore be transported through this writer by inventing one post-hoc
three-dimensional group action.

### 7. Test equivariance and predictability of the local metric field

This branch is complete in two complementary forms. Direct phase-matched
nuisance transport makes the rank-two tangent intervention work in all cells,
but the original nuisance-replicate shuffle was structurally a positive
equivalence, not a valid negative control, and random rank-two controls also
worked. A locked frozen-artifact correction uses a five-bin semantic-phase
shift and random plane as the negative controls. Equivalence and specificity
pass `3/3`, while paired tail geometry passes only seed 29. The corrected test
therefore rejects one universal nuisance-invariant identity projector at
`1/3`, without misusing the original control.

The sharper signed-covector test fit one phase-conditioned covector on source
cohorts A/B and evaluated fresh cohort C. Its fresh prediction reaches
`R2 0.989--0.996` and mean signed cosine `0.9969--0.9995`; using that frozen
covector with the fresh signed error passes all six causal cells. The local
task-sensitive direction is therefore portable conditional on the declared
phase chart even though an undirected projector is not uniquely identified.

### 8. Predict only the signed scalar residual

This diagnostic is complete. On untouched cohort E, the primary pre-write
activation observer has fresh `R2 -2.528`, `-0.387`, and `0.130` and passes no
checkpoint. Calibration, individual activation, post-MLP, output-posterior,
and combined look-ahead summaries also produce no joint predictive-and-causal
pass. Meanwhile, both the local oracle and frozen-covector/exact-scalar control
again pass all six cells. The primary activation patch passes composition in
all three checkpoints but extrapolation in none, so the small causal benefit
is support-relative rather than portable.

A no-fit type check then applies exact target-preserving scale, orientation,
offset, and composed actions. The required signed error fails invariance in
all 24 cells, with negative paired R2 throughout. This residual is the
symmetry defect of the frozen approximate writer; an invariant scalar head is
structurally mismatched to it.

The symmetric first-order decomposition localizes the changing factor to the
rank-three coordinate residual rather than the task covector. The exact
task-angle groupoid decomposition then rejects a stronger shortcut: the
writer's action defect alone explains no cell, because a material direct-state
term contributes in all 24. Thus an action-conditioned scalar remains the
right *interface dimension*, but its target must come from an observable
calibrated semantic estimate rather than a writer-only transformation law.

The registered stop rule is active: end retrospective source-only observer
fitting under these summaries. Another Fourier writer, PCA rank, ridge value,
nonlinear probe, topology scan, or residual penalty is not the next test.

### 9. Stress the calibrated reference before optimizing it

This inference-only causal curve is complete. Ten retained analytic and
learned-equivariant systems were evaluated with common-random-number reference
errors at `sigma={0,0.05,0.10,0.20,0.40,0.80}`. No model, front end, or task
head was trained.

The preregistered robustness claim fails: the analytic radius is `0.00` and the
learned radius is `0.05`, versus a required `0.20`. The mechanism is not early
loss of the quotient probe endpoint. Every representation cell in both arms
passes through `0.20`; branch accuracy and conditional log-loss pass across
the entire curve. Exact-bin task utility fails first. The learned system is
slightly more tolerant at `0.05`, but the analytic positive control also
fails, so more learned-front-end optimization is not licensed.

Single-group ablations localize the measurement dependency: orientation and

## Where link cobordism fits

Link cobordism is a secondary certification tool for the localized degree and
branch-locus program, not a replacement for the causal quotient chart. It is
well posed only after declaring a three-dimensional generator or intervention
manifold on which a regular-value preimage is a one-dimensional link. A
sublayer homotopy can then expose equivariant births, deaths, merges, or branch
events as a cobordism between adjacent-cut links.

The orbit simplices used in the `C2` and `C3` experiments are only one- and
two-dimensional, so link language does not apply to them directly. Cobordism
also cannot prove frozen-continuation sufficiency, repair observation
identifiability, or select a cross-seed gauge. Use it only when topology forces
a codimension-two singularity on a declared 3-D slice; retain Reynolds/character
coupling and causal patching as the mechanism and endpoint.

Neither the `C16` writer obstruction nor the corrected nuisance-projector tail
failure defines such a codimension-two locus, so neither result activates a
link-cobordism experiment.
