## Verdict

The declared nuisance-invariant metric-field hypothesis was **not confirmed by
the registered gate**, but that gate is control-invalid.
The complete gate passed `0/3` selected checkpoints, with the fixed
classification `nuisance_specific_metric_field` in all three.

The result is narrower than that classification name can express. Using the
canonical rank-two projector at the exactly transformed input repaired every
amplitude, orientation, offset, and composed similarity-group cell. But the
phase-matched nuisance-shuffled projector repaired every cell too, as did a
deterministic isotropic rank-two projector. The intended projector therefore
has no preregistered causal specificity at this endpoint.

The geometric distributions tell the same story. Paired kernel lines have
very high medians (`0.9913--1.0000`), yet their lower tails rotate, and the
correct correspondence never separates from the phase-matched shuffle. No one
of the 24 arm/shift/checkpoint cells passes the complete geometry gate.

The supported causal statement is:

```text
fresh transformed residual
  -> many rank-two carrier planes retain enough correction
  -> local, identity-transported, shuffled, and random rank-two patches pass
  -> the rank-two circular-moment row space is too broad to identify a
     unique task metric or symmetry transport law.
```

This reinforces the rank-one task-covector result. The next useful activation
analysis is not another rank-two topology or projector scan; it is to explain
the example-specific signed scalar multiplying the already portable
phase-conditioned task covector.

## Exact observed-group intervention

The experiment applied the target-preserving observed action

```text
g_(a, alpha, t) x = a R_alpha x + t
```

to the continuous three-channel sensor history before tokenization. Four arms
separately tested amplitude, planar orientation, constant offset, and their
composition. The group action did not use phase, target, branch, or labels.

Across all 24 transformed cells:

- clipping fraction was exactly zero;
- non-finite count was zero;
- changed-token fraction ranged from `0.2575` to `1.0000`;
- all target posterior, bin, quotient-phase, and exact-`C2` pair contracts
  remained equal; and
- all `64 x 2 x 3` continuation Jacobian fields had rank two.

The worst centered finite-difference relative error was `0.04462`, below the
locked `0.05` ceiling. Maximum tangent/kernel decomposition leakage was below
`8.41e-13`.

## Geometry

For a rank-two continuation Jacobian `J`, the test compared its carrier row
projector `P = J+ J` and the equivalent rank-one kernel line before and after
the group action.

| Statistic over 24 cells | Minimum | Maximum | Locked requirement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| median paired kernel cosine | `0.9913` | `1.0000` | `>= 0.95` |
| p10 paired kernel cosine | `0.5708` | `0.9962` | `>= 0.90` |
| p95 projector distance | `0.1020` | `0.9686` | `<= 0.4359` |
| paired-minus-shuffled median cosine | `-0.0020` | `0.0068` | `>= 0.10` |

The typical kernel line is close, especially in seed 29, but exact pairing has
essentially no advantage over another nuisance replicate at the same quotient
phase. This can mean that the task direction is genuinely stable over a wider
nuisance fiber, but it cannot identify the registered group correspondence.
The p10 and p95 failures additionally reject uniform pointwise equality.

## Frozen causal patches

For transformed residual `e_g`, the primary state used the canonical
projector `P_x e_g`; controls used the target-local projector, a
phase-matched nuisance-shuffled projector, and a deterministic isotropic
rank-two projector. All were continued through the same frozen model.

Aggregate mean circular-moment shifts from the exact state, averaged over
composition and extrapolation, were:

| Seed | predicted | local rank two | identity transported | shuffled rank two | random rank two |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 7 | `0.13781` | `0.02046` | `0.02049` | `0.02049` | `0.05916` |
| 29 | `0.16222` | `0.03164` | `0.03167` | `0.03176` | `0.07674` |
| 53 | `0.13773` | `0.01620` | `0.01623` | `0.01626` | `0.05564` |

Local, identity-transported, shuffled, and random rank-two states all pass the
full continuous endpoint in all 24 cells. The shuffled advantage over the
registered transport is at most `0.00013` bins by regime, and the random
advantage reaches only `0.0345--0.0464` bins, below the locked `0.05` floor.

This is the generic-subspace loophole in its cleanest form. In a
three-dimensional carrier, any two rank-two planes intersect in at least one
line. When the task-effective correction is nearly scalar, most such planes
retain enough of it to pass a permissive circular endpoint. Passing the causal
task does not make the complete rank-two plane identifiable.

## Relationship to prior evidence

The preceding nuisance-fiber transport study reached the same qualitative
conclusion over the broader N3 observation groupoid: source rank-two planes
were causally interchangeable but nonspecific, with failures concentrated in
extrapolation tails. The present experiment isolates an exact, declared
similarity subgroup and removes input-validity ambiguity. Its inputs and
numerics all pass, yet specificity still fails.

The scalar activation-conditioned studies provide the sharper object:

```text
portable phase-conditioned task covector g(theta)
  + nonportable example-specific signed amplitude y(x)
  -> successful local correction.
```

The rank-two result should therefore be treated as supporting context for that
decomposition, not as a separate universal metric-field interface.

Require the source-only state to pass both composition and extrapolation and

task activations contain the missing error amplitude. It does not reopen

## Method boundaries

The exact group covers scale, planar orientation, and constant channel offset,
not every N3 nuisance. Tokenization approximates the continuous group action.
Exact residual amplitudes are diagnostic and unavailable at inference time.
Every patch is local and off manifold. The carrier, writer, readout, and three
checkpoints were selected in prior work. Failure of rank-two specificity does
not reject the already measured rank-one task covector or a prospective
architectural equivariance constraint.
