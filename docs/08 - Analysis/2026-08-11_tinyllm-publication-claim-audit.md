# TinyLLM publication-claim audit

**Status:** PUSH A NARROW CAUSAL-METHODS RESULT; DO NOT PUSH THE UNIVERSAL THEORY

**Date:** 2026-08-11

**Evidence role:** synthesis of sealed reports and meta-hypotheses; no new
experimental observation

## Decision

The program has a defensible paper-sized result, but not the original broad
claim that quotient formation has one universal geometry or mechanism.

The publishable thesis is:

> Identifiability, invariant representation, causal quotient sufficiency,
> acquisition, and calibrated downstream use are distinct obligations. Exact
> group interventions can determine which obligation failed before another
> model is trained.

This is supported by prospective multi-seed interventions, frozen-checkpoint
causal patching, target-changing and shuffled controls, and exact
identifiability witnesses. The failures are part of the result: several
plausible topology, sparse-circuit, scalar-interface, and optimization
explanations were rejected under their registered gates.

Do not present the work as a new universal quotient theorem, a generally
superior TinyLLM architecture, or evidence from natural language or real
sensors.

## Evidence accounting

The current meta-hypothesis store contains `103` TinyLLM records. Every record
is marked tested: `27` have `confirmed=true` and `76` have `confirmed=false`.
These counts are governance facts, not a vote or a collection of 103
independent experiments. Several records share checkpoints, generators, or
artifact lineages, and some confirmed records are exact preflights or
post-outcome diagnostics rather than fresh population evidence.

For publication claims, evidence is therefore graded by design rather than by
the Boolean count:

| Grade | Meaning | Appropriate use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | prospective or preregistered multi-seed causal result with valid positive and specificity controls | central empirical claim |
| B | registered frozen-artifact mechanism replicated across checkpoints or shifts | mechanism support with explicit population boundary |
| C | post-outcome, corrective, single-checkpoint, or outcome-directed diagnostic | explanation or hypothesis generation only |
| Algebra | exact proof, exhaustive finite check, or analytic identity | contract, ceiling, or impossibility result; not learned-model evidence |

## Claims that survive

### 1. Observation identifiability is a necessary design contract — Grade A + Algebra

Without an orientation reference, the absolute cosine target is not invariant
under the observation gauge. After adding a phase-independent calibration
reference, analytic and learned structured front ends pass the joint
composition/extrapolation quotient gate in `5/5` seeds at both the front end
and full depth; the raw calibrated arm passes `0/5`. Information availability
alone is therefore insufficient: the architecture must expose the identified
coordinate in an appropriate type.

This supports a concrete workflow rule: prove that the target descends through
observational equivalence, or supply a reference that fixes the gauge, before
optimizing a representation loss.

Primary evidence: [calibrated identifiability](2026-08-06_tinyllm-calibrated-identifiability-causal.md).

### 2. Quotient representation and causal use are different — Grade A

In both calibrated structured arms, exact task-fiber barycenters are causally
sufficient before block-0 attention in `5/5` checkpoints on composition and
extrapolation. Semantic barycenter shuffles fail every tested cell. Natural
paired-sheet residual differences nevertheless persist and can grow with
depth.

The defensible conclusion is task-relative causal closure, not literal collapse
of the entire residual state. A representation may retain nuisance detail that
the frozen task computation no longer needs.

Primary evidence: [calibrated front-end causal closure](2026-08-10_tinyllm-calibrated-frontend-causal-closure.md).

### 3. Raw-model invariant synthesis is localized, distributed, and typed — Grade B

For the stable degree-two raw checkpoints, the exact Reynolds/Jensen defect
localizes the first quotient-sufficient sublayer in `5/5` seeds and is
shift-stable across the complete twelve-sublayer regime. Removing exact charged
deck-character carriers destroys sufficiency and restoring them repairs it in
`5/5` seeds for both tested degrees and shifts.

The mechanism is not one privileged scalar or head. In the stable C2 cohort,
no singleton or head pair is sufficient; source-fitted rank one fails all three
checkpoints, while the smallest sufficient preregistered dyadic ranks are
`2`, `8`, and `4` (later boundary refinement gives `2/5/3`). This supports a
compact but genuinely vector-valued, distributed synthesis mechanism in the
declared C2 scope.

Primary evidence:

- [Reynolds character coupling](2026-08-06_tinyllm-reynolds-character-coupling.md);
- [irrep fusion ablation](2026-08-06_tinyllm-irrep-fusion-ablation.md);
- [attention-head decomposition](2026-08-06_tinyllm-c2-attention-head-decomposition.md);
- [defect-subspace rank](2026-08-06_tinyllm-defect-subspace-rank.md).

### 4. Exact symmetry constrains the function class but does not guarantee acquisition — Grade A

The relational C3 architecture contains the exact computation in six nonzero
weights and preserves its declared equivariance at every allowed parameter
state. The analytic solution passes `5/5` fresh seeds, while the learned true
arm passes only `1/5`; no-connection, connection-shuffled, and target-shuffled
controls pass `0/5`. A post-outcome affine readout reaches `4/5` but cannot
rescue the primary result, and one failure occupies the wrong winding sector.

The supported statement is precise: identifiability and exact equivariance are
prerequisites, not guarantees of population-stable acquisition or calibrated
use.

Primary evidence: [C3 relational acquisition](2026-08-11_tinyllm-c3-relational-connection-acquisition.md).

### 5. The connection interface has an exact information boundary — Algebra + B

All analytic and frozen learned connection modules depend only on total C3
holonomy: canonicalizing seven edge connections to that representative changes
predictions by exactly zero. Erasing any one edge yields an exact
same-observation collision whose targets differ by `1.5`; no optimizer can
repair the missing information under the declared generator.

For arbitrary posterior uncertainty over total C3 connection error, one complex
character moment reconstructs the full three-state posterior and gives the
Bayes cosine and squared-error risk. The soft carrier replays through all ten
frozen module/shift cells within `2.38e-7`.

The finite-group Fourier identity is standard mathematics. Its role here is a
minimal executable interface and Bayes ceiling, not a claim that a learned
estimator has been validated.

Primary evidence:

- [connection-observation identifiability](2026-08-11_tinyllm-c3-connection-observation-identifiability.md);
- [posterior-holonomy interface](2026-08-11_tinyllm-c3-posterior-holonomy-interface.md);
- [posterior-estimator training license](../07%20-%20Status%20Reports/2026-08-11_tinyllm-c3-posterior-estimator-training-license.md).

### 6. Fixed-operator ceilings are valid negative controls on model value — Grade A

Across the C3 temporal extensions, simple declared group operators repeatedly
close the task before TinyLLM construction or retraining: an all-frame circular
mean materially beats the learned continuation, a degree-two group operator
closes constant acceleration, robust deletion closes single-frame corruption,
and charged-character scoring closes the apparent switching-law gap.

This does not prove that transformers are unnecessary in general. It shows how
to test whether a proposed learned component pays rent on a known synthetic
law. On these generators, it often does not.

Primary evidence:

- [fixed-operator ceiling](2026-08-11_tinyllm-c3-temporal-fixed-operator-ceiling.md);
- [constant-acceleration operator](2026-08-11_tinyllm-c3-constant-acceleration-fixed-operator.md);
- [single-frame robust estimator](2026-08-11_tinyllm-c3-single-frame-corruption-fixed-estimator.md);
- [charged-character fixed decoder](2026-08-11_tinyllm-c3-charged-character-fixed-decoder.md).

## Claims that were falsified or narrowed

| Rejected claim | Decisive evidence | Surviving narrower statement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| quotient formation has one universal Morse–Cerf normal form | mature basins and controls are seed/shift dependent; autonomous Morse gate fails `0/5` | low-dimensional landscape events can accompany, but do not define, a causal front |
| predictive barycenter closure identifies causal quotient closure | predictive closure appears earlier than frozen causal sufficiency | prediction and causal continuation must be tested separately |
| degree `k` determines the lowest synthesis order | C3 admits neutral quadratic `c1*c2`; cubic dominance fails | the lowest charge-neutral combination of characters actually present controls allowed order |
| one head, head pair, or rank-one direction implements the C2 front | all singleton/pair and rank-one gates fail | synthesis is distributed but occupies a compact task-effective subspace |
| the whole residual becomes a nuisance quotient | natural sheet differences persist; raw whole-state barycenters fail | a task-sufficient projection or carrier may close while nuisance detail remains |
| exact equivariance solves learning | analytic `5/5`, learned `1/5` in the exact relational class | symmetry removes invalid functions but leaves winding, scale, and optimization sectors |
| a portable invariant scalar or small writer repairs downstream use | observer, writer, transport, and cross-seed gates fail | downstream use is checkpoint- and chart-conditioned unless typed prospectively |
| better loss under averaging is quotient evidence | Jensen convexity explains more than the observed final-query gain | causal posterior preservation is required |

## What would be trivial or overstated

The following are not standalone contributions:

- observing that group averaging creates invariant features;
- restating finite-group Fourier selection rules;
- deriving a C3 posterior mean from three categorical probabilities;
- showing that an analytic canonicalizer solves a synthetic generator it was
  designed from;
- counting confirmed meta-hypotheses as independent replications;
- treating post-outcome readout repair as confirmation of a failed primary;
- presenting synthetic exact-orbit membership as a deployable intervention.

The nontrivial contribution is the controlled separation of feasibility,
representation, causal use, acquisition, and calibration—and the demonstrated
ability of that separation to stop unnecessary training.

## Recommended paper boundary

One coherent methods paper can be built around four figures:

1. **Identifiability intervention:** uncalibrated impossibility, calibrated raw
   failure, and structured `5/5` recovery.
2. **Causal quotient intervention:** orbit-barycenter replacement versus
   semantic shuffle at front-end and depth cuts.
3. **Raw synthesis mechanism:** propagated versus actual barycenter, charged
   carrier ablation, head distribution, and task-effective rank.
4. **Architecture-versus-acquisition boundary:** exact relational class and
   analytic `5/5` ceiling versus learned `1/5`, followed by the holonomy
   information boundary.

The fixed-operator ceiling sequence belongs in a compact “model value” section
or appendix. The topology, Morse, cobordism, scalar-writer, and corrective
readout branches should appear as a falsification ledger rather than competing
headline stories.

## Remaining validation required for a broad claim

Before claiming generality beyond this controlled synthetic program, obtain at
least one of:

- a second independently designed task family with a different nuisance group;
- a real or externally motivated sensor/reliability stream;
- prospective replication across a materially different transformer family;
- a deployment task where a learned typed estimator beats fixed Bayesian and
  low-dimensional adaptive controls.

None is currently present. Until then, the paper should claim a causal
methodology and bounded mechanistic findings, not universal representation
theory.
