# Experimental Finding — Wavelets Were Not in the Executed Router

The ten-experiment campaign successfully built both an embedding-proximity
router and a custom competence-wavelet basis, but the final routed campaign used
the former before the latter existed. Consequently:

- the 30.97% non-nesting result is valid A/B/C behavior evidence;
- the 3.91% cost reduction is a valid result for generic SmolLM/PCA proximity;
- it does not determine whether competence-wavelet proximity can identify the
  complementary success regions;
- Experiment 11 supplies the missing out-of-sample connection and evaluates it
  without recycling the revealed PAWS test outcome.

This finding supersedes any interpretation that Experiment 10 tested the full
task-wavelet routing hypothesis.

## Follow-up result

Experiment 11 supplied the missing connection. On held-out development
partition 3, the competence-wavelet router improved accuracy from 77.02% to
77.66%, but raised mean proxy cost from 150.94 to 152.73 and escalated more
often. The original hypothesis therefore receives limited support for
competence representation, but not for cost-efficient routing with the current
frozen A/B heads.
